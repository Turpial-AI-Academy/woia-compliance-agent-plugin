# Compliance contract


Legal/Compliance owns this capability; another department requests a distinct competent contribution. The plugin does not invent legislation, applicability, professional opinion, policy values or authority. A host supplies accepted applicable versioned policy, exact current scope and attributable evidence.

Review compares supplied accepted fact values to policy-owned allowed-value criteria. Missing evidence/facts remains insufficient; a mismatch does not become an automated legal decision. Decisions and exceptions require named competent exact policy-bound evidence, immutable unique records and accepted outcomes/rules. Hold is immutable and scoped; retention resolution blocks disposal whenever a hold exists. This initial implementation has no hold-release action: resolve through a separately accepted future contract, not by deleting history.

Without holds, retention evaluates a policy-owned retain-until timestamp. Eligibility is not disposal authority; Documents must enforce its own protected retention/disposal effect. No professional decision is created by a model confidence score, template criterion, historical role or technical access.

Operations/history remain durable JSON state in the qualified host atomic store; no law dataset or database is chosen. Unit tests use synthetic policy and named competent decision fixtures, not legal advice or operator qualification.
